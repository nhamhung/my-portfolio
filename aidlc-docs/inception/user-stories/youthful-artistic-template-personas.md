# Personas - Youthful Themes and Artistic Student Template

## Persona 1: Younger Student Author

### Profile

A secondary or high school student who has coursework, interests, hobbies, clubs, experiments, and personal goals but may have little or no employment history or formal recognition.

### Goals

- Present curiosity, growth, and personality without pretending to have professional experience.
- Treat schoolwork, personal projects, clubs, and creative activities as meaningful portfolio material.
- Create a polished portfolio by editing a small number of clear data files.
- Choose a presentation that feels expressive, age-appropriate, and credible.

### Needs

- Artistic must prioritize interests, current learning, hobbies, activities, and goals.
- Empty Experience or Awards data must not create empty navigation or awkward blank sections.
- Real formal achievements must remain available when they exist.
- Copy must be encouraging without sounding childish, corporate, or fabricated.
- Images and motion must support the student's actual work and remain readable on school or mobile devices.

### Relevant Stories

- YAT-US-03: Build an honest Creative Notebook portfolio through Artistic.
- YAT-US-04: Maintain student-life content and handle sparse formal sections.
- YAT-US-05: Select and retain a preferred portfolio style.
- YAT-US-06: Receive an accessible, responsive, and dependable experience.

## Persona 2: Student Portfolio Author

### Profile

A student or recent graduate who maintains shared profile, education, project, journal, skill, and contact data and wants several distinct but manageable ways to present it.

### Goals

- Choose a presentation matching their subject, personality, and audience.
- Keep one shared content foundation rather than maintaining four separate sites.
- Use youthful color and layout without weakening readability or credibility.
- Change the source default safely and understand where Artistic-specific content lives.

### Needs

- Neutral, Business, and Artistic must feel structurally and visually distinct from Engineering and each other.
- Neutral and Business palettes must feel energetic without covering long-form reading surfaces in saturated color.
- Artistic-specific content must live in one typed and documented data file.
- Existing routes, actions, local journals, color modes, and layout modes must remain reliable.
- Engineering must remain a stable default and regression baseline.

### Relevant Stories

- YAT-US-01: Express a learning journey through Youthful Neutral.
- YAT-US-02: Organize student work through Youthful Business.
- YAT-US-03: Build a Creative Notebook presentation through Artistic.
- YAT-US-04: Maintain shared and Artistic-specific content.
- YAT-US-05: Provide four valid presentation choices.
- YAT-US-06: Preserve shared quality and Engineering stability.

## Persona 3: Portfolio Visitor

### Profile

A classmate, teacher, mentor, admissions reviewer, collaborator, recruiter, family member, or community visitor who opens the portfolio on desktop or mobile.

### Goals

- Understand what the student enjoys, is learning, has made, and hopes to do next.
- Explore available content without needing the student to have formal work experience.
- Compare presentation styles without losing the current section or journal post.
- Use navigation, actions, and controls comfortably with keyboard, touch, or reduced-motion preferences.

### Needs

- Every template must provide clear identity, hierarchy, navigation, and readable contrast.
- Artistic must reveal actual student media and content rather than decorative filler.
- The Portfolio style selector must expose four clear choices and preserve context.
- Empty sections, overlapping controls, clipped text, and unintended horizontal scrolling must not appear.
- Color, animation, and collage composition must not interfere with comprehension or focus.

### Relevant Stories

- YAT-US-01: Read Youthful Neutral comfortably.
- YAT-US-02: Scan Youthful Business efficiently.
- YAT-US-03: Understand a younger student's Creative Notebook.
- YAT-US-05: Select and persist a portfolio style without losing context.
- YAT-US-06: Navigate an accessible and responsive portfolio.

## Maintainer Constraints

Maintainer concerns remain acceptance constraints rather than a separate persona:

- Keep App ownership, section IDs, hash routes, layout state, local journal routing, and static deployment stable.
- Keep Artistic-specific content typed, local, and separate from component implementation.
- Scope every template's CSS and color variables.
- Add no runtime dependency, backend, CMS, authentication flow, or custom scrolling system.
- Preserve automated checks and explicit Engineering regression coverage.
